Reading this map

Lines reveal a network. Underflow shows waterways mapped in OpenStreetMap within a rectangular Nairobi study area.

Deep-blue lines are tagged as rivers, teal as streams, and muted violet as drains, ditches, or canals. Dashed lines have explicit intermittent or seasonal tags. A solid line does not prove year-round flow.

Each segment is an OpenStreetMap way clipped to the study area. Kilometres are calculated along those mapped lines. Named segments with the same name and type are grouped for browsing; that does not establish a connected hydrological system.

The Flood timeline separates historical accounts beginning in 1961, reported incidents, dated satellite observations and a long-term model baseline. Blue polygons show the available 1 May 2024 UNOSAT observation or partial 14/26 April 2025 GFM detections. Report pins are approximate references with individual dates and sources, not inundation boundaries. March 2026 is reported history. The separate Model baseline chapter has no event date and is not a 2026 forecast. Missing geometry does not establish dry ground.

This is not a historical reconstruction, flood-risk assessment, water-quality map, or engineering survey. Missing lines and tags mean missing information, not necessarily missing waterways.
